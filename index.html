The House

Atelier

For the moments when simply getting dressed isn't enough.

House of Liraé is a custom occasionwear house built around the belief that what you wear should feel as unforgettable as the moment itself.

Founded by designer Akela Walker, the name Liraé was created from a combination of letters from her late grandmother’s name. A tribute to the woman who was always her greatest supporter. Her belief in Akela now lives within the name of the House and everything created under it.

Each piece begins with the individual, her vision, her presence, and the way she wants to feel when she enters the room. Through sculptural silhouettes, intentional details, and statement-making design, each garment is created to feel distinctly her own.

Crafted with intention. Created for impact.

The Process

Five steps
01

Consultation

Your moment. Your vision. Your story.

We begin with you, your occasion, inspiration, personal style, and how you want to feel when you enter the room.

02

Design

Where the vision takes form.

Your ideas are translated into a custom design through silhouette, fabric, color, texture, and intentional details.

03

Creation

From concept to construction.

Your garment comes to life through patternmaking, draping, construction, and the details that make the piece distinctly yours.

04

Fittings

Refined for you.

Through a series of fittings, the garment is shaped and adjusted to achieve the intended fit, proportion, and silhouette.

05

Final Look

Made for your moment.

The finished piece is revealed, complete, considered, and ready for the moment it was created for.
